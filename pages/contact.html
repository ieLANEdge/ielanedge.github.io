Common Questions

Frequently asked

How quickly do you respond to inquiries?+
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours on business days. For urgent matters, please mention it in your message and we'll prioritise accordingly.
Do you work with clients outside India and MENA?+
Absolutely. While India and MENA are our home markets, we work with clients globally. Our remote-first team is equipped to serve clients in any timezone.
What's the typical project timeline?+
It depends on scope — an MVP might take 6-10 weeks, while enterprise transformation projects can span several months. We'll give you a clear timeline during discovery.
Can ieLAN Edge invest in my startup?+
Yes — we offer strategic investment and co-building partnerships for startups aligned with our mission, particularly in the India-MENA corridor. Visit our Ventures page to learn more.
How do I apply for a job at ieLAN Edge?+
Visit our Careers page to see open roles and apply directly, or send an open application to careers@ielanedge.com — we review every submission.
